For October 30, use the [current readiness snapshot](CURRENT-DEMO-READINESS.md) and refresh it against [progress](OPERATOR-SNAPSHOT.md) immediately before presenting. Prefer a useful, truthful five-minute demonstration. A failed or unavailable step moves to retained evidence; it does not trigger repair commands during the presentation.

1. **Explain the purpose (30 seconds).** “Inertia turns an organization's existing knowledge and objectives into coordinated work. Paperclip's Coordinator oversees funding, meetings and general work; funding is our first end-to-end target.” Open the [proof map](architecture/index.html).
2. **Show existing knowledge (60 seconds).** In the member-owned `/work` app, use a known organization question such as the previously confirmed DATACU example. Show the answer and source references. Say: “The app can retrieve this corpus. Connecting the funding agents to this same scoped reader remains an integration step.” A source chip is not an immutable artifact-review receipt.
3. **Show discovery and accountability (60 seconds).** If Paperclip is healthy, open the retained Alpha handoff/Coordinator queue: 13 bindings, provenance and priorities. Explain the capacity blocker frankly. Do not generate new tasks solely for stage effect. If UI is unavailable, show the dated retained report with its timestamp.
4. **Show independent critique (60 seconds).** Open Charlie's retained rejection and the automatic same-case Beta correction continuation. Explain what was missing substantively. Never describe rejection as acceptance or a completed application.
5. **Show the actual output (60 seconds).** Open the retained brief and, if operator-provided access exists, its Markdown/DOCX render. Say: “This is a generated pursuit brief with eight typed fields. Application narrative, project plan, budget and final independent packet review are unfinished.” Three render calls represent one content revision, not three applications. Do not improvise a protected file URL or imply a native DOCX attachment exists.
6. **Close with the next useful outcome (30 seconds).** Restore reliable execution, fan out the retained portfolio, and turn evidence into reviewed application material; connect the existing corpus reader and notes through their owning interfaces. Humans retain exact-version authority and current manual submission.
