## Operating flow

1. Read authorized active objectives, projects, geography and applicant facts. Query current scoped partner/program evidence first.
2. Maintain two queues: due or changed known-source rechecks, and bounded searches for uncovered government, corporate, foundation, sponsorship and in-kind opportunities.
3. Validate promising leads at the official source. Bind sponsor, program and cycle to captured evidence, source/hash and observation time. Record exact cutoff/timezone when published; retain date-only uncertainty and failed coverage explicitly.
4. Compare benefit with application, delivery and reporting effort separately. Assign the supported direct, partner, next-cycle, monitor or standing-benefit route and an actionable next step.
5. Persist useful scoped evidence through existing candidate/enrichment and trusted knowledge services. Return a durable delta/receipt or explained no-change result. Retain reusable discoveries from rejected opportunities.
6. Deliver a short prioritized brief with source/as-of, change, benefit/effort, deadline, exact action and responsible role. Send independently validated time-critical findings through a separate urgent channel with deduplication and recovery.
7. Persist due rechecks, unresolved coverage and follow-up work in Paperclip. Reuse current evidence on the next run; refresh changed or stale facts.

A useful official opportunity can reach evidence-backed review before a reusable feed adapter exists. Recurring source ingestion requires separately qualified access, extraction, identity, freshness and reconciliation. Existing enrichment, human-decision and submission authority remain unchanged.

## Activation requirements

- Task 5: assign the research agent, authorized objectives, actual search/browser tools, source access and query/page/time/cost budgets. Reserve capacity for deadline rechecks and consequential source failures; rotate uncovered project/benefit/geography cells.
- Task 6C: connect search and official evidence capture to existing candidate admission and trusted query-first writeback. Keep source, audience, freshness and scope checks at retrieval and persistence.
- Task 6D: qualify partial coverage, source failure, restart, graph lag and uncertain delivery. Persist a continuation when the budget ends. Measure detection-to-alert latency and missed cases against a declared test set.
- Task 8: connect objective-linked benefit/effort briefs, urgent delivery and native due-recheck work. Deduplicate by organization, stable opportunity/cycle and material event/revision; update only for changed facts or a newly actionable urgency threshold.
- Complete the bounded rehearsal, agree cadence and alert/recheck service targets, then authorize and configure recurring execution. The daily schedule remains disabled until these gates pass.

## Acceptance

1. Find an unseeded official opportunity, including a near-deadline positive, using the actual assigned agent and search tools.
2. Exercise search → official validation → candidate/enrichment → scoped writeback → brief/urgent delivery → next-run reuse; report observed latency and missed cases.
3. Cover changed deadlines, exact and date-only cutoffs, new listings, future openings, closed cycles, standing benefits and partner-dependent routes.
4. Preserve a generic failed official-page probe as unknown coverage, triage/recheck it, and allow separately verified urgent evidence to surface without marking the failed adapter successful.
5. Restart mid-run and after uncertain delivery; retain durable work and avoid duplicate alerts. Cover budget exhaustion, partial pagination, unavailable tools, source outage and writeback failure.
6. Retrieve useful rejected-opportunity knowledge on the next run and deny revoked or superseded sources immediately.

## Ownership

Paperclip owns agents, budgets, execution, review and follow-ups. DFW owns opportunities, canonical evidence, eligibility and application state. Inertia connects source/host adapters and shared delivery. Helix supplies the derived partner/program index. Source provenance, prospect/collaborator status and scoped authority remain attached to every result.
