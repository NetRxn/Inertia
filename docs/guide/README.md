# Inertia MVP guide

[Project README](../../README.md)

Inertia helps an organization turn existing knowledge and objectives into accountable work. Paperclip coordinates tasks and review; the member app makes organization knowledge and work accessible. Funding is the current MVP workflow, with a general Coordinator rather than a grant-only product.

- [Use Inertia](user-guide.md): find a case, inspect evidence and read proposed work.

- [Install and qualify](install.md): source setup, plugin packaging and native composition.
- [Release and recover](release.md): exact-source checks, deployment, rollback and tags.
- [Source integration](../development/source-integration-20261029.md): integrated branches and validation receipts.

## What the MVP demonstrates

Saved Alpha candidates reached the Coordinator and thirteen distinct Beta case tasks. Four case providers ran concurrently; later cases progressed while earlier cases awaited independent source review. Scoped organization search supplied source/passages/hashes to actual saved writing, with readback and Markdown/DOCX parity. Clean-primary rendering and editorial review are demonstrated mechanisms; they do not establish consumer acceptance of the latest application. See the [clean-render deployment record](../releases/clean-application-20261030/README.md) for the explicit output/review status.

Charlie independently rejected sources and returned corrections to the same case. FUTO source proposal `393f…` was accepted, completed its native source-review child and resumed Beta. **Source approval is not approval of the application draft, budget or final packet.** No final Charlie whole-packet approval or external submission is claimed.

The Coordinator has saved a ranked shortlist of three active opportunities in its existing queue, preserving all thirteen case bindings. The [P2 evidence record](../releases/p2-shortlist-20261030/README.md) binds that result to its actual native document revision and body hash. This closes the ranked-handful outcome; individual case and draft evidence does not establish completed portfolio or application acceptance. Full 1,475-record coverage is not an MVP gate. Missing legal/contact facts and final authority remain explicit; answerable proposed writing can proceed before those final decisions. The separate source-reconciliation audit is retained evidence, not a demand to restart the funding scan.

## Deferred from this MVP

Real Paperclip↔Buzz participation, Gemini-note ingestion/action triage, and durable exhausted-case/new-evidence-only revisit are follow-up work. Member-app chat/knowledge success does not prove a live Paperclip Buzz bridge. Notes code/tests do not establish import into the canonical DAO notes store. A human-readable Markdown draft is acceptable; rigorous application-quality regression and whole-packet acceptance remain separate work.

Historical architecture, fixture receipts and the original product PDF are preserved. [Delivery snapshots](../delivery/CURRENT-DEMO-READINESS.md) and investigation charts describe dated engineering state. Use the source-integration record and operator's current release receipt for current identities; do not infer deployment from a Git merge or tag.
