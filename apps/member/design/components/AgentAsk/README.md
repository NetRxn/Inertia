The agent's ask is the single highest-value input Inertia needs from this person right now — the heart of the app.

- Markup: an `in-ask in-glass` panel with `in-ask__head` (presence `in-dot`, "Inertia" label, a time chip), `in-ask__body` (a `signal` GlassIcon, `in-ask__title`, `in-ask__why`), an `in-ask__ctx` block of `in-mono` lines, and `in-ask__actions`.
- The consumer provides: the ask as a short imperative title; why this person (one sentence, based on what they've picked up or said); context lines (initiative, source, due, credit); an effort estimate.
- Actions, always these three in this order: primary "Take it", secondary "Not now", quiet "Suggest someone". Declining is never penalized and never needs a reason.
- One ask in view at a time. Others wait in a quiet "More asks (3)" link.
- The agent's words stay in grotesk and mono; any quoted member line inside uses `quote` (serif italic).
