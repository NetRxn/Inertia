An idea card holds one member's idea in their own words, where it came from, and how much agreement it has.

- The people realm, so it is a **hearth**, not glass: `hearth` ground, `hearth-edge`, a light `in-tex--weave`, and a walnut `in-prairie` double rule above the text — the warmth of a Wright den.
- Markup: an `in-idea in-tex in-tex--weave` card with `in-idea__src` (a small GlassIcon + `in-mono` source line, and a status chip), `in-prairie`, `in-idea__text` (the idea, `idea` style), and `in-idea__meta` (avatar, name, time, agreement chip).
- The consumer provides: the idea text, verbatim (never truncated below two lines); the author's first name and initials; the source ("telegram · #events", "meet · wed"); a time; the agreement count; its status.
- Status chips: `in-chip--pollen` "New", `in-chip--tide` "Needs confirm" (captured by the agent, not yet confirmed), `in-chip--ion` "Drafted" (the agent turned it into a motion). Agreement: `in-chip--moss` "+4".
- Never rank ideas with red/green scores — an idea is never "losing".
