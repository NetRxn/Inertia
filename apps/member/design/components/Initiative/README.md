An initiative is the organization's planet: a ratified goal with its projects as moons in a slow, steady orbit.

- Markup: an `in-init in-glass` card holding an `in-init__sys` SVG (a `tide` planet with a `moss` ring, `line-strong` orbits, moons in an `in-init__orbit` group) and a text column (`in-label in-init__eyebrow`, `in-init__title`, `in-init__desc`, chips in `in-init__stats`).
- The consumer provides: the initiative's name and one-line status, and its projects as moons — `moon--done` (moss), `moon--now` (signal, at most one), `moon--next` (hollow). Size a moon by the project's scope, 5–9px.
- The orbit turns once every 90 seconds — steady, never attention-seeking; off under reduced motion.
- Sit it on a starfield (`in-tex--stars`): the organization lives in space. Use it on the home view and initiative pages, one per row.
- Keep its language diplomatic: "ratified by quorum", "needs hands", never "behind" or "at risk".
